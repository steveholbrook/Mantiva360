The Mantiva360 product

Follow the project position to the work behind it.

Mantiva360 connects planning, delivery, progress, evidence, resources, costs, forecasts and risks in one control context. Programme leaders can start with the exception, inspect its cause and decide what needs an authorised response.

Why is it off track?

Trace every signal to its source.

Buyer problem: executive status is often separated from the records and rules that produced it.

Supported capability: the 360 Cockpit projects current Project Health, reconciliation and strategy-aware measures into one review surface. It does not create a second calculation path.

Practical workflow: filter the view, open a finding, inspect its impact and follow the affected record into the authorised product workflow.

Can I explain the reported progress?

Recognise progress through the configured control method.

Buyer problem: effort, spend, evidence and progress can be treated as if they mean the same thing.

Supported capability: Mantiva360 keeps these signals distinct. The configured control method governs progress recognition. Evidence can support assurance, while actual effort and cost remain actuals.

Practical workflow: inspect the finding, confirm the source criteria and evidence, update the governed record through an authorised user, then review the recalculated result.

Important control boundary

Uploading evidence, recording time or allocating cost does not by itself earn progress or close an exception.

Where is delivery at risk?

Keep the timeline connected to execution.

Buyer problem: a programme timeline can look orderly while ownership, deliverables, evidence or next actions remain unclear.

Supported capability: the Delivery Plan relates workstreams, activities, timing, ownership, deliverables and evidence within the same project context.

Practical workflow: select an activity, inspect its dates and progress context, then review the linked tasks, resources, deliverables and evidence relevant to the decision.

Control before explanation

Different records answer different questions.

Mantiva360 connects the records without collapsing their meaning. Configuration, access and source quality still govern the usefulness of the result.

Plan and forecast

Baseline and forecast records describe commitments and the expected path forward. They remain distinguishable from actual delivery.

Record actuals

Approved actual effort and cost allocation describe what was consumed. They do not automatically create recognised progress.

Recognise progress

The configured control method governs progress recognition, supported by the relevant acceptance and evidence context.

Verified capability coverage

A connected model for planning, control and review.

Activities and Tasks & Backlog

Connect scheduled activity to the execution detail, ownership and next work behind it.

Scope & Deliverables

Resources and assignments

Planning & Forecasts

Keep baseline, current plan and forecast information available for variance and outlook review.

Actuals and allocation

Reporting
