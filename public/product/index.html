01 / Detect

Where are we losing
money or time?

The 360 Cockpit brings Project Health and reconciliation findings into a focused review surface. Inspect cost, progress, schedule and resource context, then follow the finding to its source.

360 Cockpit · Project Health · RAID

02 / Understand

What is causing it?

Review the affected record alongside baseline, current forecast, actuals and risk. A signal helps you locate a problem; project judgement establishes the cause.

Use forecasts to examine remaining effort and cost-to-complete. Keep potential exposure separate from committed cost and validated savings.

Forecasts · Actuals · Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies

03 / Look ahead

What happens if
we do nothing?

Inspect the current outlook, remaining work and resource demand. Follow activities into tasks, scope and deliverables to understand where dates, capacity or acceptance need attention.

Delivery efficiency depends on realistic work and capacity assumptions, not an individual employee productivity score.

Delivery Plan · Tasks · Scope & Deliverables · Resources

04 / Get to Green

What can we change?

Make recovery an explicit decision. Assess the current position, compare responses and retain the assumptions behind the chosen route.

Assess the position

Keep original commitments, the approved baseline and the current forecast distinguishable. Surface gaps in the inputs rather than treating unknowns as zero.

Compare the response

Use Forecast, Delivery Plan and Change Request scenarios to examine modelled impacts. Confirm the cause, owner, due date, assumptions and residual risks.

Choose the authority

Route the response to source correction, recovery within authority, formal change or sponsor escalation. Retain the decision and its rationale.

A modelled efficiency opportunity is conditional on the assumptions and successful execution. It is not a validated saving.

05 / Act

What will the
change affect?

Link recovery to the existing Change Request workflow when commitments need to change. Assess, approve and implement through authorised roles. Keep corrective actions accountable with a required outcome.

Change · Corrective actions · Scenario modelling

06 / Verify

Did the intervention work?

Reassess the source conditions and review the new outlook. Completing an action or approving a change alone does not prove recovery.

Refresh the assessment

Get to Green checks the current position again. Source changes and stale assessments require rechecking; the original recovery position remains visible.

Explain the result

Prepare project reporting from canonical results. Review the narrative and supporting records before sharing the position with executives.

Spend less time assembling

Connected reports and read-only AI assistance help authorised users interpret permitted records and reduce repetitive preparation.

The trust underneath

Controls calculate. People decide.

The result should be explainable all the way back to the records and assumptions that produced it.

Progress has a method

The configured control method governs progress recognition. Time, cost or an evidence upload does not automatically create earned progress.

Calculations stay traceable

Deterministic calculations and reconciliation establish the reported measures. AI explanation remains separate from the calculation path.

Authority stays explicit

Your authorised team owns changes and the underlying condition must be resolved before the finding can close. Source quality and freshness matter.
