Readiness with evidence

A phase label is not a quality decision.

Readiness review needs the relevant plan, deliverables, acceptance, risks and evidence. Mantiva360 can keep those records connected, while the authorised governance process retains the decision.

Quality-gate publication boundary

This site does not claim a generally available automated quality-gate workflow. The exact gate records, approvals and operating responsibilities must be verified for the intended deployment.

Connected, not universal

Keep specialist systems in their proper roles.

Use the evaluation well

Bring questions that expose the operating model.

Which record is authoritative?

Confirm where plans, actuals, progress, deliverables and risks originate and who is authorised to change them.

Which rule recognises progress?

Test the configured control method against representative delivery and evidence scenarios.

What closes an exception?

Follow a finding to its source and confirm that action completion does not substitute for resolving the condition.
