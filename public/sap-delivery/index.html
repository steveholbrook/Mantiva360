The six-phase journey

Make performance visible
through every phase.

Use SAP Activate phase context in Mantiva360 to organise the work and review its delivery economics. The examples below describe project-management uses, not SAP system execution.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Frame the investment

    Clarify the business case, delivery scope and assumptions before committing.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Establish the baseline

    Connect workstreams, project planning, resources and delivery cost.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Make scope visible

    Track fit-to-standard activities, decisions and deliverables in the plan.

  4. 04

    Realize

    Track delivery economics

    Review build, testing and data preparation work alongside progress, actuals and forecast.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Focus on readiness

    Follow cutover activities, dependencies, issues, adoption and remaining work through hypercare.

  6. 06

    Run

    Review and improve

    Track transition actions, project closure and lessons for continuous improvement.

Phase names and fit-to-standard terminology checked against official SAP guidance. Read SAP’s methodology overview.

Where to use it

The moments when delivery
economics matter most.

Apply the same connected project-management model to the work your SAP programme needs to coordinate.

S/4HANA implementation

Review fit-to-standard scope, delivery commitments and resource demand before they become forecast surprises.

ECC to S/4HANA transition

Organise migration workstreams, dependencies and validation deliverables. Track the project effort and cost around the transition.

Testing and data preparation

Track activities, remaining work, acceptance and issues. Keep the delivery consequence of repeated cycles visible.

Cutover and business adoption

Coordinate readiness activities, dependencies, decisions and unresolved issues in the project plan.

Hypercare and transition

Review outstanding delivery obligations, resource demand and transition actions as the programme moves into operations.

Programme recovery

Compare the current outlook with recovery scenarios. Route approved changes and reassess the result with Get to Green.

Specialist systems execute testing, migration and technical cutover. Readiness and quality decisions remain with the accountable programme team.
