Privacy

Clear about your data.

Website delivery, videos and enquiry handling.

Current website state

Contact Mantiva360 by email. Sales, demo requests, general enquiries and privacy questions go to sales@mantiva360.com. There is no active enquiry form on this website.

Email links open your email application. Opening a link does not send a message or submit an enquiry. You choose whether to send the email.

Email enquiries

When you send an email to sales@mantiva360.com, Mantiva360 receives your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your name, organisation and project question.

Include only the information needed to answer your enquiry. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a meeting or create a purchase.

Videos

The two local MP4 films are served from this website and load in the player only after you select them. They do not contact YouTube.

The website uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain. The video frame is not loaded until a visitor actively chooses to play a video. When a video is loaded, Google and YouTube may receive technical information and apply their own privacy terms.

Caption and transcript verification remains open for the existing YouTube content and the supplied local films. Burned-in text alone does not establish full video accessibility.

Visitors can choose to open the same content directly on YouTube.

Technical website data

Firebase Hosting and its infrastructure may process standard request information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, time, requested page, browser information and response status.

This review build does not include marketing analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural profiling or a cookie banner. If analytics or additional third-party services are introduced, this notice and the consent approach must be reviewed before they are enabled.

Retention, access and deletion

This website does not store enquiry submissions. Messages you choose to send are handled through Mantiva360's email service. Email handling is separate from the public website.

For questions about how your enquiry is handled, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact sales@mantiva360.com.

Privacy contact

Email sales@mantiva360.com for privacy questions and requests. This is also the contact address for sales and general website enquiries.
