The sensible objections.

Isn’t this just another dashboard?

That is the right challenge. Evaluate whether you can move from a finding to the relevant records and an accountable response. A prettier summary alone is not a sufficient reason to add software.

Will we have to replace our existing tools?

Do not assume a wholesale replacement. Assess the project-control gap and your operating model, including which records belong in Mantiva360 and which remain in specialist systems. Verify each import or integration separately.

Does evidence automatically earn progress?

No. The configured control method determines how progress is recognised. Supporting evidence, actual effort and cost have distinct roles; uploading a file or logging hours does not automatically earn progress.

Does AI decide whether the project is healthy?

No. Deterministic services calculate health and reconciliation. The authorised assistance is read-only and helps interpret permitted information. It does not rewrite project records or independently remediate delivery.

What will we need to set up?

An agreed control method, relevant project records, accountable owners and reporting settings. Test the setup and ongoing update effort on a representative project rather than assuming maintenance will be negligible.

How do we assess enterprise readiness?

Confirm access boundaries, hosting, data handling, retention, audit, support and operational responsibilities during evaluation. This page does not assert unverified certifications, residency commitments or native connectors.
