01 / The economic problem

Cost drifts before the finance report turns red.

The early signals are scattered across delivery. The cost appears when they go unconnected for too long.

Capacity without progress

Committed effort is being consumed while delivery falls behind.

A growing cost-to-complete

Remaining effort increases while the approved budget stands still.

Slippage and rework

Late decisions, dependencies and repeated work extend the delivery window.

Risk turning into cost

An emerging issue or scope change alters the outlook before the report catches up.

Status chasing

Project managers reconcile competing updates instead of leading the response.

Unrealistic allocations

Demand exceeds available capacity, making an apparently workable plan fragile.

The focus is the delivery system: work, demand, commitments and constraints.

07 / How it works

Detect. Decide. Act. Verify.

A connected path from an emerging condition to an accountable response.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Surface cost, progress, resource, schedule and risk signals.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Inspect the source, confirm the cause and compare recovery scenarios.

  3. 03

    Act

    Apply authorised corrections or take the decision through change control.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Recalculate the position and check whether the underlying condition improved.

Controls calculate. People decide.

Deterministic calculations, governed source records and reconciliation keep the result traceable. Progress follows the configured control method. Evidence, spend and effort have distinct roles. AI assistance helps interpret permitted records; authorised people own the decision.
