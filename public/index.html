Product story

From signal to source to accountable action.

Mantiva360 does not change a dashboard to manufacture a better status. It connects a material signal to its source and the action needed to address the underlying condition.

  1. 01Signal

    See the material signal.

    Begin with a prioritised control finding, not a manually assembled headline.

  2. 02Source

    Understand why it was flagged.

    Inspect the affected record, impact, owner and reason before changing anything.

  3. 03Action

    Act on the underlying condition.

    Correct the authoritative record, retain accountability and review the recalculated position.

Representative fictional project data. All three frames come from the same captured reporting state; no different projects or reporting periods are combined.

Buyer questions

What should a guided review prove?

Choose one disputed control question and inspect the records, rules and decisions behind it.

Does Mantiva360 replace SAP Cloud ALM, Jira or Microsoft Project?+

No. Mantiva360 is positioned as a project-control and assurance layer alongside the systems used to execute work. Data exchange and native integration must be assessed separately.

How is project progress governed?+

The configured project-control strategy determines how progress is recognised. Actual effort or cost does not automatically create earned progress.

What does the AI do?+

AI supports interpretation and navigation of authorised project records. Core calculations, health checks and reconciliation remain deterministic and explainable.

How does Mantiva360 align with SAP Activate?+

Projects can use phase classification across Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy and Run. Mantiva360 is independent and does not claim SAP endorsement or certification.

What happens when I open the demo?+

The product destination is separate from this marketing site and may require sign-in or approved access. Anonymous editing and automatic reset are not promised.

Is Mantiva360 production-ready for my organisation?+

That cannot be answered by a marketing page. A responsible evaluation must verify the intended release, security controls, permissions, operating model, support and deployment evidence.
