Controlled corrective action

Find the issue. Understand the cause.
Make the correction. Check the result.

Mantiva360 does not make a red project green by changing a dashboard. It points the team back to the source records and then recalculates the control position.

  1. 01
    Find the issue

    Start with the prioritised exception, not a manually assembled status deck.

  2. 02
    Understand the cause

    Inspect the affected delivery, scope, resource, financial or evidence record.

  3. 03
    Make the correction

    Update the authoritative source with ownership, reason and audit context.

  4. 04
    Check the result

    Rerun deterministic controls and confirm whether the underlying condition changed.

This is a controlled fictional scenario, not a promise that every real project becomes green through one action. Real status depends on the quality, timeliness and governance of the underlying data.

Straight answers

Questions worth asking before a demo.

Mantiva360 should earn its place by improving control, not by adding another reporting burden.

Does Mantiva360 replace SAP Cloud ALM, Jira or Microsoft Project?+

No. Mantiva360 is positioned as a project-control and assurance layer alongside the systems used to execute work. Any data exchange or native integration must be assessed and verified separately.

How is project progress governed?+

The configured project-control strategy determines how progress is recognised. Actual effort or cost does not automatically create earned progress. PM assessment, tasks, deliverables and evidence are used according to the implemented strategy and its governance rules.

What does the AI do?+

AI supports interpretation and navigation of authorised project data. Core calculations, health checks and reconciliation remain deterministic and explainable. The assistant is not positioned as an autonomous project manager.

How does Mantiva360 align with SAP Activate?+

Projects can use SAP Activate phase classification and phase-aware delivery views across Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy and Run. Mantiva360 is independent and does not claim SAP endorsement or certification.

What happens when I open the demo?+

The demo destination is separate from this marketing site and may require sign-in or approved access. Only fictional demonstration data should be used. Anonymous editing and automatic reset are not promised.

What should a guided review cover?+

Choose one real control question, such as forecast confidence, disputed progress, delivery readiness or exception management. The review can then focus on the records, controls and decisions behind that question.
