Connect the SAP delivery picture

Read narration and visual description

Visual description: Connected plans, progress, resources, actuals, forecasts and risks lead into recorded Cockpit findings and corrective-action views. The team changes the governed source before recalculation. Progress options are distinguished. SAP Activate is shown as delivery context. No successful recovery or quantified saving is depicted.

Audio: Narration with instrumental background music.

On a complex SAP programme, status can become a weekly chase across schedules, spreadsheets, forecasts, RAID logs and email. When the signals disagree, leaders lose time debating the report instead of acting on delivery.

Mantiva360 brings the project-control picture together. Plans, progress, resources, actuals, forecasts and risks are reviewed in one connected context.

Start with attention. The 360 Cockpit surfaces material findings and shows which dimension is affected. Then ask why. Follow the reason-coded condition back to the governed record behind it.

Move from cause to action. See the owner, target date, required outcome and expected impact. The team makes the change in the authorised source. Mantiva360 reruns deterministic health and reconciliation checks, so the result is inspected rather than assumed.

The Delivery Plan connects activities, tasks, deliverables, evidence and dependencies. Progress follows the project's configured control method. Evidence and accountable assessment support confidence, but neither time nor cost automatically creates earned progress.

For SAP delivery, work can be viewed in an SAP Activate phase context without replacing the tools teams already use.

Built-in health and reconciliation agents highlight exceptions. Read-only AI helps authorised users understand their project data and the next decision.

Mantiva360. Know what needs attention, and why. Data to Progress. Learn more at mantiva360.com.
