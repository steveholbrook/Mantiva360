The principle

More delivery. Less waste.

Help programme leaders identify avoidable cost and delivery inefficiency while there is still time to respond.

Controls, evidence and reconciliation are the foundation for credible information. The purpose is better project decisions: understanding the driver, modelling the recovery, governing the change and verifying the result.

SAP is our primary delivery context. The underlying performance questions also matter across complex enterprise transformation.
