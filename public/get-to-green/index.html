01 / Recognise the warning signs

Projects rarely fail without warning.

The warning signs are often visible long before recovery becomes expensive.

Major transformation programmes generate enormous amounts of delivery information. Yet leaders can struggle to establish a reliable view of progress, completion costs and emerging risks.

By the time a programme is formally Red, schedule disruption, additional resource costs, contractual exposure and declining confidence may already be affecting delivery commitments.

Schedule slippage

Missed milestones and delayed business benefits.

Cost escalation

Increasing forecast completion costs.

Resource constraints

Reduced delivery capacity and productivity.

Unresolved dependencies

Blocked activities and downstream disruption.

Weak evidence and governance

Unreliable progress and acceptance reporting.

Fragmented recovery planning

Corrective actions disconnected from financial forecasts.

The costliest problem is not always the original delay. It is the time lost before effective corrective action begins.

Adding resources or extending dates can change cost, schedule, margin and business outcomes. Evaluate delivery cost and schedule implications before committing capacity or changing delivery commitments. Commercial judgement remains with your authorised team.

07 / Get to Green in action

See the recovery process in action.

From critical findings to evidence-supported recovery decisions.

Follow the condition from its source to an accountable response. These product captures use demonstration data; the films above show the recovery assessment workflow.

  1. 01 / Identify

    Find what needs intervention.

    Review critical findings and Project Health in the 360 Cockpit. Follow the affected record to understand the condition behind the signal.

  2. 02 / Assess

    Open the recovery assessment.

    In the 360 Cockpit, open the Panoramic Cockpit and choose Assess recovery. Use Confirm position to assess saved source records and review qualifications.

  1. 03 / Understand

    Establish cost and schedule exposure.

    Distinguish original commitment, the approved baseline at recovery entry and the current outlook. Review unavailable inputs before relying on a financial comparison.

  2. 04 / Compare

    Assess the response before committing.

    Use Compare options with linked Forecast, Delivery Plan or Change Request scenarios. Review proposed expenditure, finish dates, capacity and assumptions. Unmodelled options retain the current outlook.

  1. 05 / Execute

    Give the response authority and ownership.

    Use Approve response to establish the route. Maintain actions and evidence in their registers. Changes to approved commitments follow the existing Change Request approval process.

  2. 06 / Verify

    Recheck the result and remaining exposure.

    In Track recovery, choose Verify recovery now. Review current health, source conditions, action evidence and remaining constraints. A changed or stale position requires reassessment.
