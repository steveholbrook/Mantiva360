## Financial and media qualifications

Both supplied films visibly contain REVIEW CUT markings and demonstrate a draft, low-confidence, unverified position with missing financial inputs. They illustrate the workflow rather than an achieved financial improvement or verified project recovery. The supplied thumbnail is retained as the financial film poster. Do not describe these assets as a completed customer recovery.

The page avoids unsupported customer outcomes, industry certifications, automatic savings and SAP endorsement. EVM measures depend on the configured progress method and complete eligible inputs. Evidence uploads and Actuals do not independently create earned value.
