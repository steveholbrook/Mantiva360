# Mantiva360 product-led design review

Prepared 17 September 2026 for selection before any production homepage change.

## Review objective

Increase product visibility and make Mantiva360 feel easier to understand before a prospect reads detailed capability copy. Each direction keeps the approved Mantiva360 identity, uses genuine product captures with representative fictional data and sends every **Get started** action to `https://mantiva360.app/`.

The review route is `/design-review` and is marked `noindex,nofollow`. It is intended for an isolated Firebase preview channel only. The current production homepage is unchanged on this branch.

## Concepts

| Option | Direction | Best quality | Principal trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Product panorama | Strongest product-first sales story and clearest visual demonstration | Requires the best high-resolution capture set to reach full potential |
| 02 | Executive split | Fast executive comprehension while retaining a familiar Mantiva360 silhouette | Less distinctive than the centred concepts |
| 03 | Guided showcase | Best explanation of the attention, cause, action and impact journey | More structured and less dramatic above the fold |
| 04 | Control theatre | Highest perceived premium and strongest contrast for the product screen | Dark presentation may feel heavier for conservative buyers |
| 05 | Boardroom canvas | Most differentiated editorial treatment for executive audiences | The layered screens need careful mobile simplification |

## Recommendation

Select **Option 01, Product panorama** as the production direction. It gives the interface the visual weight the current homepage lacks, keeps the story simple and supports capability switching without turning the page into a catalogue. Use Option 03's question-led labels inside the selected direction.

## Selection boundary

Selection approves the presentation direction, not every word or capture. The production pass should still:

- replace any capture that lacks sufficient resolution at the selected display size;
- test the first viewport at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels;
- preserve keyboard access, reduced motion and readable focus states;
- confirm the application destination works for signed-out prospects; and
- keep claims within the existing content and claims register.
