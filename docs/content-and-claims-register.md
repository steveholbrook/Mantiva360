## Excluded claims

- customer testimonials, customer logos, adoption counts, quantified return on investment, awards or pricing without approved evidence;
- SAP endorsement, certification, partnership or an unverified native integration;
- universal replacement of SAP Cloud ALM, Jira, Microsoft Project or financial systems;
- single source of truth, perfect information, zero setup or guaranteed outcomes;
- expenditure, time entry or evidence upload automatically creating earned progress;
- autonomous remediation or assistant write access;
- SOC 2, ISO certification, single sign-on, data residency or enterprise-scale tenancy without product evidence;
- patent, patent-pending or filing claims without approved wording and verifiable details; and
- a successful enquiry, confirmed meeting or instant demo access without a verified accepting service.
